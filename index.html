01 — The Farm

A controlled-environment farm in the Lamphun countryside.

Every batch is grown, harvested, and recorded under a single, traceable standard — from clone to cure.

Set in Pa Sang district, Lamphun, our facility combines open-air cultivation beds with climate-controlled grow rooms, giving us year-round consistency without losing the character of the northern highlands.

Rose of the North Biosciences Co., Ltd. was built as a compliance-first operation: fenced and access-controlled grounds, dedicated propagation, vegetative and flowering rooms, and post-harvest handling that follows GACP documentation from seed to sale.

We work with processors and researchers looking for a dependable, well-documented Northern Thai supply — grown under a name, and a rose, that stands for where it's from.

02 — The Standard

What GACP means on our farm.

Good Agricultural and Collection Practices — the WHO-modelled framework for medicinal plant quality — applied to every stage of cultivation.

Cultivation

Sourced & grown

Genetics, propagation, and growing conditions are logged from clone through to harvest, so every plant's history can be traced back to its mother stock.

Collection

Harvest & handling

Harvest timing, drying, and curing follow written procedures designed to protect cannabinoid and terpene quality batch after batch.

Records

Traceability

Inputs, environmental data, and staff activity are recorded on-site, giving partners a documented chain of custody for every delivery.
